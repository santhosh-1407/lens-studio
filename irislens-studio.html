04 · Viva Preparation

Walk in prepared.

Tap a question to reveal a short answer you can explain aloud.

Why is Template Matching not scale invariant?＋
The template is compared at one fixed size. If the object appears larger or smaller, the pixel pattern changes and the correlation score drops.
Why are integral images useful in Viola-Jones?＋
An integral image lets the sum of any rectangle be computed with just four array lookups, which makes Haar feature evaluation extremely fast.
What does DeepFace do?＋
It detects and aligns the face, extracts deep features with a neural network, and compares the representations to verify whether two faces belong to the same person.
What is triplet loss in FaceNet?＋
Triplet loss pulls the anchor and positive images closer in embedding space while pushing the negative image farther away, by at least a margin.
